CrossCopy - Free online clipboard to share text between devices

CrossCopy sends text between your phone, tablet and computer in real time. Paste text on one device, open the same link or scan the QR code on another, and the text appears instantly. It runs in any web browser on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux, with no app to install and no account.

How it works

  1. Open crosscopy.xyz. You get a new clipboard with its own link.
  2. Paste or type your text.
  3. On your other device, open the same link or scan the QR code. The text syncs as you type.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. CrossCopy is a website and works in any modern browser.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free, with no sign-up.

Who can see my text?

Anyone who has your clipboard link. Use the random link for sensitive text and clear it when you're done.

Can I pick my own link?

Yes. Type any name after the domain, for example crosscopy.xyz/yourname.
