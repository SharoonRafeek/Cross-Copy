# Clipboard Sync Troubleshooting Guide

> A quick troubleshooting flow for slow or missing text updates.

Source: https://www.crosscopy.xyz/docs/clipboard-sync-troubleshooting
Published: 2026-02-07 · Updated: 2026-02-07

## Fast diagnostics

Confirm all devices are on the exact same route URL.

Refresh stale tabs and verify internet connectivity on each device.

## Common fixes

Close duplicate tabs that may show outdated content.

Try a new route to rule out stale session issues.

Disable aggressive privacy extensions temporarily if updates are blocked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do I see old text?

Most often a stale tab or wrong route URL is open on one device.
