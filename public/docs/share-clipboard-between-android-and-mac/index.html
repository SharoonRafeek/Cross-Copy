Share Clipboard Between Android and Mac

A practical Android-to-macOS clipboard bridge that works instantly in the browser.

5 min read · Published 2026-02-07 · Updated 2026-02-07

Common Android and Mac limitation

Apple Universal Clipboard does not include Android devices.

A neutral browser tool fills that gap with no vendor lock-in.

Setup

Create or open a CrossCopy route on Android.

Open the same route on your Mac and pin it in a browser tab for repeat use.

Paste text on one side and copy from the other device immediately.

Best practices

Use custom route names that are memorable but not guessable.

Clear old content after transfers to reduce accidental exposure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I share long code snippets?

Yes, but keep formatting checks in mind after pasting into editors.

Is this only for Chrome?

No. It works across modern mobile and desktop browsers.
