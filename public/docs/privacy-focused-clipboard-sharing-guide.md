# Privacy-Focused Clipboard Sharing Guide

> Use route discipline and minimal data exposure to reduce privacy risks.

Source: https://www.crosscopy.xyz/docs/privacy-focused-clipboard-sharing-guide
Published: 2026-02-07 · Updated: 2026-02-07

## Privacy principles

Share the least amount of text required for the task.

Prefer one-time routes for temporary operations.

## Operational habits

Rotate route names for different contexts.

Never post active route URLs in public chats or tickets.

Clear route content when the transfer is complete.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can teams use this safely?

Yes, with clear internal policy on route sharing and content sensitivity.
