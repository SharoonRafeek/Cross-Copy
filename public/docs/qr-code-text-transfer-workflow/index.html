QR Code Text Transfer Workflow

Fast route handoff from desktop to mobile with QR scan.

3 min read · Published 2026-02-07 · Updated 2026-02-07

When QR is best

QR codes are ideal when URLs are long or you want to avoid typing on mobile.

They are also useful in demos or quick pair sessions.

Workflow

Open a CrossCopy route on desktop.

Scan the QR code with your phone camera.

Start sharing text instantly in the same route.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a third device too?

Yes. Multiple devices can open the same route.
